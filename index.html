04 — Questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We ask in plain language what takes up your time and build around it. The finished system is run from an admin panel that needs no coding.

What does it cost, and how long does it take?

Both depend on scope. After the brief you receive a fixed written package with the price, so you know the cost before any work begins.

What happens after launch?

We stay close through launch to fix what real use turns up. After that you manage content, users and payments yourself.

Who is behind Vestmoor?

Vestmoor is deliberately a house, not a personality. Every message, proposal and delivery comes from the team as one.

Do you work outside a single country?

Yes. Vestmoor is built for a global market and works remotely with clients wherever they are.
